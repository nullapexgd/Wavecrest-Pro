In developmentWavecrest 3.0

A rebuilt site and a stricter service

  • New design, with a real-data chart of a channel you can explore in 3D and flatten into a plot.
  • Free beta stated plainly: one analysis a month with an account. Pricing, privacy and terms pages rewritten to match what the product does.
  • Reports are private to your account by default, with an optional share link.
  • The monthly allowance is counted on the server, one run at a time, and a run that fails on our side is not counted.
  • Sign-in only accepts email addresses that Google or GitHub has verified.
  • Errors from YouTube and the language model are no longer passed through to the page.

Accounts, plans and API keys

  • Sign in with Google or GitHub, with plans and API keys per account.
  • A five-stage progress display with live counts while an analysis runs.
