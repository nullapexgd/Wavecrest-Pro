Questions

What counts as one analysis?

One run on one channel, reading up to its 100 most recent public videos. A run that fails on our side is not counted against your month.

Can I try it without signing in?

You can explore the chart on the home page. It uses real public data from a channel picked at random, and you can choose another. A real analysis needs an account, so the free allowance cannot be used up by anyone else.

Does it show my private YouTube Studio numbers?

No. Wavecrest only reads public data. Watch time, retention and revenue are private to the creator, so a report marks them unavailable and labels any earnings figure as an estimate.

Is the free beta really free?

Yes. There is no card, no trial that converts, and no purchase flow. If that ever changes, you will be told here first.
