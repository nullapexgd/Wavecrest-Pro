Privacy.

Short version: we keep your account, your reports and the counts needed to enforce the monthly limit. We run no ad trackers and sell nothing.

Last updated 28 October 2026

What we collect

Account
When you sign in with Google or GitHub we store your email address (verified by the provider), name, profile picture link, which provider you used, and your plan. We never see your Google or GitHub password.
Session cookie
One cookie, wc_session, keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax. It is not used for tracking.
Channels you analyze
The channel you submit, and the public channel and video data YouTube returns for it (titles, descriptions, dates, view, like and comment counts, durations). No private data and no YouTube account access.
Reports
The result of each analysis, linked to your account. Reports are private by default. A report can be opened by others only if you turn on its share link.
Usage records
For each run we keep a timestamp, status and the channel key, so we can enforce one analysis a month and avoid charging failed runs.
Feedback
If you answer "Was this report useful?", we receive your answer, your message, your account email and the report's ID, by email. It is not shown to anyone else.
API keys
If you create one, we store a hash and a short prefix. The full key is shown once and cannot be recovered.
IP address
Used only for per-hour rate limits. It lives in Upstash for the length of the window and is not tied to your account or reports.

The home page chart

The chart shows public upload data for a well-known or smaller YouTube channel, picked at random or by you. It comes from YouTube's public feeds and the YouTube Data API, is cached on our servers, and is the same for every visitor. Choosing a channel sends nothing about you to YouTube. These creators have no connection to Wavecrest.

Reelcut (cutting your own videos)

Reelcut works on a video file you choose on your own computer. The file is read by your browser and is never uploaded. The speech in it is turned into text on your device by a speech model that your browser downloads once (from Hugging Face, which can see your IP address when it does) and keeps in its cache.

To pick the best moments, we send the transcript text of the part of the video you chose, and the note you gave, to our server and on to Groq, or to Google Gemini if Groq is unavailable. We do not store that text, the clips or the video. Their own policies cover what those providers keep. Clips are then cut on your device.

We keep one counter per account of how many videos you have used this week. It expires within nine days. Only use footage you own or have the right to use.

What we do not do

  • No advertising cookies, no analytics trackers and no third-party scripts on this site.
  • No selling or renting your data.
  • No access to your YouTube account, Studio data, comments or private videos.

Who processes data for us

Vercel
Hosts the site and the API.
Neon
Stores accounts, reports and usage records (PostgreSQL).
Upstash
Short-lived rate-limit counters and cached sample data.
Google (YouTube)
The channel you analyze is looked up through the YouTube Data API, so YouTube receives that request. Wavecrest uses YouTube API Services; by using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. A report also shows video thumbnails, which your browser loads directly from YouTube's image servers (i.ytimg.com), so Google can see your IP address when you open one.
Groq
Runs the language model. The public video titles, descriptions and statistics of the channel being analyzed are sent to it to write the report. If you use Reelcut, the transcript text of the part you chose is sent to it too. Your email and name are not.
Google (Gemini)
Used as a fallback when Groq is unavailable, for the same text Groq would receive. Your email and name are not sent.
Hugging Face
Serves the speech model that Reelcut downloads to your browser. It receives that download request, including your IP address. It receives no video, audio or text from you.
Resend
Sends the confirmation email if you use the early-access form. The address is not stored by us.

How long we keep it

Reports and account details stay until you delete them or ask us to. Following YouTube's API rules, a daily job removes the YouTube statistics (view and like counts, medians, subscriber counts and the revenue estimate built from them) from a report once it is 30 days old. The analysis text is kept, and the report says so. We do not otherwise delete reports automatically. Rate-limit counters expire within an hour. Cached sample data expires within a week.

Your choices

  • Revoke an API key at any time.
