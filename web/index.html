Every claim says how we know it.

A report separates what YouTube shows from what we compute, infer and estimate. Nothing implies access to a creator's private analytics.

Observed
Read directly from public YouTube data, such as a title or a view count.
Calculated
Computed from observed data, such as a median or an upload cadence.
Inferred
A model's reading of the evidence. It cites the videos it relied on and can be wrong.
Estimated
A range built on stated assumptions, such as ad revenue.
Unavailable
Private to the creator, such as watch time. We say so instead of guessing.
